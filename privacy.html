Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to AI Maths Tutor. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and protect student information when you use our educational web application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: When you register or sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your display name and email address.
  • Google Authentication: We only access basic identity scopes (openid, email, profile). We do not access your Google password, contacts, Google Drive, or any other private service.
  • Educational Curriculum: Selected NCERT grade (e.g. Class 9, 10, 11, 12) and teaching style preference to personalize math problem-solving.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To personalize step-by-step mathematical learning based on your NCERT curriculum.
  • To securely manage your authenticated chat sessions and history.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade student data with any third-party advertisers.

3. Data Protection & Security

Passwords are protected with Bcrypt cryptographic hashing (never stored as plain text). Authenticated sessions are secured via industry-standard signed JWT tokens. All network interactions are encrypted over HTTPS / TLS.

4. User Rights (India DPDP Act 2023)

Users have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of their account and conversation history at any time through the in-app Settings menu or by contacting our developer administration.
